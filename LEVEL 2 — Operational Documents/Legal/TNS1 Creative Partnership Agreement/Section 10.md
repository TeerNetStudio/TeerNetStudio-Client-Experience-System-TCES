# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 10 — Copyright & Ownership

---

## 10.1 Purpose

This section defines the ownership, usage rights, and intellectual property associated with creative work produced under this agreement.

Its purpose is to ensure both the Client and the Designer clearly understand who owns what, when ownership transfers, and how creative assets may be used.

---

## 10.2 Ownership Before Final Payment

Until the project has been paid in full, all concepts, drafts, previews, source files, and working files remain the intellectual property of **TeerNetStudio**.

During this stage, preview files are provided solely for review and approval purposes and may not be used commercially, published, or redistributed.

---

## 10.3 Ownership After Final Payment

Upon receipt of full payment, ownership of the **final approved deliverables** transfers to the Client, unless otherwise stated in the approved Project Scope or Quotation.

The Client may then use the final deliverables for their intended business or personal purposes.

---

## 10.4 Source Files

Source files are **not automatically included** with every project.

If source files are included in the agreed Project Scope or purchased separately, they will be delivered after full payment.

Examples include:

* Adobe Photoshop (.PSD)
* Adobe Illustrator (.AI)
* Adobe InDesign (.INDD)
* Adobe After Effects (.AEP)
* Canva Editable Files
* Figma Files
* Other editable project files

If not specifically included, only the agreed export formats will be delivered.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

Many projects only require final exported files (such as PNG, JPG, PDF, or MP4). Editable source files are a separate deliverable and should be requested before the project begins.

---

## 10.5 Portfolio Rights

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, TeerNetStudio reserves the right to display completed public work within its portfolio for the purpose of showcasing creative experience.

This may include:

* Portfolio website
* Social media
* Behance
* Dribbble
* Client presentations
* Award submissions

Confidential or unreleased work will never be shared without the Client's permission.

---

## 10.6 Third-Party Assets

Some projects may include licensed or third-party assets such as:

* Stock images
* Fonts
* Icons
* Illustrations
* Mockups
* Audio
* Video

Ownership and licensing of these assets remain subject to their original license agreements.

The Client is responsible for obtaining any additional licenses required for extended commercial use unless otherwise agreed.

---

## 10.7 Client-Supplied Materials

The Client confirms that they have the necessary rights to use all materials provided for the project.

This includes:

* Logos
* Images
* Videos
* Audio
* Brand assets
* Trademarks
* Documents
* Written content

TeerNetStudio is not responsible for copyright claims arising from materials supplied by the Client.

---

## 10.8 Unauthorized Use

Preview files, draft concepts, rejected concepts, or unpaid work may not be:

* Published
* Sold
* Redistributed
* Modified
* Used commercially
* Claimed as final work

without written permission from TeerNetStudio.

---

## 10.9 AI & Creative Tools

TeerNetStudio may use industry-standard creative tools, including AI-assisted tools, to improve workflow efficiency where appropriate.

Regardless of the tools used, the final deliverables remain subject to the quality standards, review process, and responsibilities defined in this agreement.

If a project requires AI-free creation or has specific restrictions regarding AI usage, this must be agreed upon in writing before the project begins.

---

## 10.10 Moral Rights

Nothing in this agreement limits either party's legal rights where applicable under intellectual property laws.

This agreement is intended to clarify commercial ownership and usage rights within the scope of the project.

---

## 10.11 Commercial Usage

Unless otherwise specified in the Project Scope or Quotation, the Client receives the right to use the final approved deliverables for their intended commercial or personal purpose after full payment.

Transfer, resale, relicensing, or redistribution of the original editable files is not permitted unless expressly agreed.

---

# Why This Matters

Creative work is intellectual property.

Clearly defining ownership protects both the Client and the Designer, ensuring there is no uncertainty about how the work may be used, shared, or modified after project completion.

Transparent ownership policies build trust and help prevent future disputes.

---

## 📌 Important Note

Owning a final design does not automatically include ownership of every working file, draft, licensed asset, or creative process used to produce it.

The exact deliverables are always defined within the approved **Project Scope (TNS-002)** and **Quotation (TNS-004).**
