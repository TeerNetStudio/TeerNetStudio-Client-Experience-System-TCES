# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 11 — Confidentiality & Privacy

---

## 11.1 Purpose

Creative projects often involve sharing confidential business information, unpublished ideas, branding strategies, and digital assets.

This section explains how such information is handled and protected throughout the partnership.

---

## 11.2 Confidential Information

For the purpose of this agreement, confidential information may include, but is not limited to:

* Business strategies
* Brand guidelines
* Marketing plans
* Unreleased products or services
* Login credentials
* Project files
* Source files
* Financial information shared for the project
* Client databases
* Internal documents
* Any information clearly identified as confidential

---

## 11.3 Designer Commitment

TeerNetStudio agrees to handle confidential information responsibly and use it solely for the purpose of completing the agreed project.

Confidential information will not be intentionally disclosed to third parties without the Client's permission, unless required by law.

---

## 11.4 Client Commitment

The Client agrees not to publicly share:

* Internal quotations
* Draft concepts
* Unreleased project discussions
* Confidential business practices
* Internal documentation

without prior permission from TeerNetStudio.

This helps protect both parties and preserves the integrity of the creative process.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

Great partnerships are built on trust. Respecting each other's confidential information protects ideas, businesses, and long-term relationships.

---

## 11.5 Access to Project Files

Where access to accounts, platforms, or cloud storage is required:

* Access should be limited to what is necessary for the project.
* Credentials should be shared securely.
* TeerNetStudio recommends changing passwords after project completion if sensitive accounts were accessed.

---

## 11.6 Data Storage

Project files and communication records may be securely retained for administrative, backup, and future support purposes.

However, TeerNetStudio **cannot guarantee** indefinite storage of project files.

Clients are strongly encouraged to maintain their own backups of all delivered files.

---

## 11.7 Public Portfolio

Completed projects may be displayed in the TeerNetStudio portfolio **only after they have been publicly released**, unless:

* the Client requests confidentiality in writing,
* the Project Scope specifies otherwise, or
* a separate Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) has been signed.

---

## 11.8 Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA)

If a project requires a formal NDA, both parties may sign a separate NDA document before the project begins.

Where an NDA exists, its terms will take precedence over this section in relation to confidential information.

---

## 11.9 Security

TeerNetStudio takes reasonable measures to protect digital project assets and communications.

However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

Both parties are encouraged to follow good cybersecurity practices when sharing files and information.

---

## 11.10 Privacy

Personal information collected during the project will only be used for purposes directly related to:

* Communication
* Project management
* Invoicing
* Payment processing
* Legal compliance

TeerNetStudio does not sell or intentionally share Client personal information with unrelated third parties.

---

## 11.11 End of Partnership

The confidentiality obligations described in this section continue to apply even after the project has been completed, unless otherwise agreed in writing or required by law.

---

# Why This Matters

Creative projects often begin long before they become public.

Respecting confidentiality protects business ideas, intellectual property, and the trust that allows successful collaborations to happen.

Privacy and professionalism are essential parts of every partnership.

---

## 📌 Important Note

Confidentiality protects both the Client and the Designer.

Mutual trust allows creative work to be shared openly, reviewed honestly, and developed with confidence.
