# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 13 — Agreement Acceptance

---

## 13.1 Agreement Acceptance

By accepting this Creative Partnership Agreement, both the Client and TeerNetStudio acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to the terms outlined in this document.

This agreement represents the shared understanding of how the project will be managed, communicated, and delivered.

Acceptance may be provided through:

* Digital Signature
* Physical Signature
* Email Confirmation
* Written Confirmation via an agreed communication platform
* Any other mutually agreed method

---

## 13.2 Entire Agreement

This document, together with the approved Project Scope (TNS-002), Quotation (TNS-004), and any written amendments agreed by both parties, forms the complete agreement between the Client and TeerNetStudio regarding the project.

Any previous discussions, verbal conversations, or informal messages are superseded by the final approved documents where applicable.

---

## 13.3 Amendments

No modification to this agreement shall be considered valid unless agreed upon by both the Client and TeerNetStudio.

Approved amendments may be documented through:

* Updated Agreement
* Written Email
* Signed Addendum
* Updated Project Scope

---

## 13.4 Severability

If any provision of this agreement is found to be unenforceable or invalid under applicable law, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

---

## 13.5 Governing Law

This agreement shall be interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws of the jurisdiction in which TeerNetStudio operates, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

## 13.7 Digital Agreement

Where permitted by applicable law, digital acceptance of this agreement shall carry the same intent as a handwritten signature.

---

## 13.8 Acknowledgement

By proceeding with the project, both parties confirm that they:

* Have read this agreement.
* Understand its contents.
* Agree to work together professionally.
* Intend to fulfil their respective responsibilities throughout the project.
