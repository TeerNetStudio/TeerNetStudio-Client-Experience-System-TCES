# 📘 TNS-001 - Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 2 — Partnership Standards

---

## 2.1 Our Commitment

At TeerNetStudio, we believe every successful creative project is built on collaboration rather than transactions.

This partnership is founded on:

* Trust
* Transparency
* Professionalism
* Respect
* Open Communication
* Creative Excellence

Both the Designer and the Client agree to work together with honesty, mutual respect, and a shared commitment to achieving the project's goals.

---

# 2.2 Client Bill of Rights

Every Client working with TeerNetStudio has the right to:

### ✔ Professional Treatment

Be treated with respect regardless of project size or budget.

---

### ✔ Clear Communication

Receive honest updates regarding project progress, delays, or challenges.

---

### ✔ Transparency

Know exactly what is included and excluded before work begins.

No hidden costs.

No surprise charges.

---

### ✔ Fair Pricing

Receive pricing that matches the agreed project scope.

---

### ✔ Creative Collaboration

Provide feedback throughout the approved revision process.

---

### ✔ Privacy

Have confidential files, business information, and project discussions handled responsibly.

---

### ✔ Timely Delivery

Receive the completed work within the agreed timeline unless affected by revisions, missing assets, unforeseen circumstances, or changes requested by the Client.

---

### ✔ Respect

Be heard.

Questions, concerns, and ideas are always welcome.

---

# 2.3 Designer Bill of Rights

TeerNetStudio also reserves the following rights.

---

### ✔ Creative Freedom

To make professional creative decisions that improve quality while respecting the Client's vision.

---

### ✔ Respectful Communication

To work in an environment free from abusive language, harassment, threats, discrimination, or inappropriate behaviour.

---

### ✔ Fair Compensation

To receive payment according to the agreed payment schedule.

---

### ✔ Healthy Working Hours

TeerNetStudio is committed to delivering quality work.

Emergency requests may be accepted, but availability outside normal working hours cannot be guaranteed.

---

### ✔ Portfolio Rights

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, completed projects may be displayed within TeerNetStudio's portfolio after public release.

(Client confidentiality is always respected.)

---

### ✔ Project Refusal

TeerNetStudio reserves the right to decline projects involving:

* Illegal activities
* Hate speech
* Adult content
* Copyright infringement
* Fraud
* Discrimination
* Harmful or misleading content
* Projects conflicting with personal or ethical values

---

### ✔ Scope Protection

Major changes outside the approved Project Scope may require:

* Additional time
* Additional quotation
* New agreement

---

# 2.4 Shared Responsibilities

A successful creative partnership requires effort from both parties.

Both agree to:

* Communicate honestly.
* Respect agreed timelines.
* Respond within a reasonable timeframe.
* Keep discussions professional.
* Review deliverables carefully before approval.
* Work together toward the project's success.

---

# 2.5 Partnership Philosophy

TeerNetStudio does not view Clients as "orders."

We view every project as an opportunity to build long-term professional relationships based on trust, quality, and shared success.

Our goal is not simply to deliver designs.

Our goal is to create work that both the Client and the Designer are proud to stand behind.

---

# 2.6 Core Values

```text
Professionalism

Transparency

Quality over Quantity

Respect over Ego

Communication before Assumptions

Solutions before Problems

Partnership over Transactions
```
