
# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 4 — Client Responsibilities

---

## 4.1 Shared Success

Creative work is most successful when both the Client and the Designer actively contribute throughout the project.

To ensure a smooth experience, the Client agrees to fulfill the following responsibilities during the project lifecycle.

---

## 4.2 Provide Accurate Project Information

The Client agrees to provide complete and accurate information before the project begins, including but not limited to:

* Project objectives
* Target audience
* Brand guidelines (if available)
* Preferred style or references
* Required text/content
* Logos and brand assets
* Images and media
* Required dimensions/platform
* Any mandatory requirements

Incomplete or incorrect information may affect the final outcome or project timeline.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

The clearer your project brief is, the faster and more accurately your vision can be brought to life.

---

## 4.3 Timely Communication

The Client agrees to respond to important questions, approvals, and feedback within a reasonable timeframe.

Delayed responses may result in adjustments to the estimated delivery schedule.

---

## 4.4 Consolidated Feedback

Whenever possible, feedback should be collected and shared in **one organized message** rather than multiple messages over several hours or days.

Example:

✅ Preferred

```text
• Increase logo size
• Change background to blue
• Replace image 2
• Fix spelling in heading
```

❌ Not Preferred

```text
10:00 AM - Change logo

11:15 AM - Change colour

2:00 PM - Actually change font

7:00 PM - Move text

11:30 PM - Remove shadow
```

Providing consolidated feedback helps reduce confusion and speeds up revisions.

---

## 4.5 Review Deliverables Carefully

Before approving any draft, the Client agrees to carefully review:

* Spelling
* Grammar
* Phone numbers
* Email addresses
* Website URLs
* Prices
* Dates
* Social media handles
* QR Codes
* Overall content

Approval indicates that these details have been reviewed by the Client.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

A careful review before approval helps prevent unnecessary delays and ensures accurate final deliverables.

---

## 4.6 Provide Legal Rights

The Client confirms that they have the necessary rights or permission to use any materials they provide, including:

* Logos
* Images
* Fonts
* Videos
* Audio
* Brand assets
* Trademarks
* Written content

The Designer cannot be held responsible for copyright issues related to client-supplied materials.

---

## 4.7 Respect the Approved Scope

Requests that significantly change the approved concept, deliverables, or project direction after work has begun may require:

* Additional time
* Revised pricing
* Updated Project Scope

---

## 4.8 Respect Agreed Timelines

The Client acknowledges that project timelines are based on timely approvals and communication.

Delays in providing required materials or approvals may result in corresponding adjustments to the delivery schedule.

---

## 4.9 Professional Communication

The Client agrees to maintain respectful and professional communication throughout the partnership.

TeerNetStudio maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward:

* Harassment
* Threats
* Hate speech
* Discrimination
* Personal attacks
* Abusive or offensive language

Constructive feedback is always welcome and encouraged.

---

## 4.10 Payment Responsibilities

The Client agrees to complete payments according to the agreed quotation and payment schedule.

Final deliverables may be withheld until all outstanding payments have been received.

---

## 4.11 Approval Responsibility

Once a design is marked as **Approved** by the Client, it is considered accepted.

Requests that introduce new concepts or major changes after approval may require additional quotation and scheduling.

---

## 4.12 Collaboration Commitment

The Client agrees to work collaboratively with the Designer by:

* Sharing honest feedback.
* Respecting agreed processes.
* Communicating openly.
* Supporting timely decision-making.

This partnership works best when both parties contribute toward the same creative goal.

---

# Why This Matters

Creative projects are a collaborative process.

The Designer is responsible for creating professional work, while the Client plays an equally important role by providing accurate information, timely feedback, and clear approvals.

When both parties fulfill their responsibilities, projects are completed faster, more accurately, and with a better overall experience for everyone involved.

---

## 📌 Important Note

These responsibilities are not intended to create restrictions.

They exist to ensure that every project is completed with clarity, fairness, and the highest possible quality.
