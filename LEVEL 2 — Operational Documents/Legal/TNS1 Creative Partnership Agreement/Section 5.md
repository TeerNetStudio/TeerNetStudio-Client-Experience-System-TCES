# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 5 — Designer Responsibilities

---

## 5.1 Our Commitment

TeerNetStudio is committed to delivering professional creative services with integrity, transparency, and respect.

Every project is approached with the same level of dedication, regardless of its size or budget.

---

## 5.2 Professional Standards

The Designer agrees to:

* Deliver work that aligns with the approved Project Scope.
* Maintain a high standard of quality and attention to detail.
* Follow professional design practices.
* Respect agreed deadlines whenever reasonably possible.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

Quality takes time. Our goal is not simply to finish quickly—but to deliver work we're both proud of.

---

## 5.3 Honest Communication

If any issue arises that could affect the project timeline or deliverables, TeerNetStudio will inform the Client as soon as reasonably possible.

Examples include:

* Technical issues
* Illness
* Unexpected emergencies
* Missing project information
* Delays caused by third-party services

Transparency is always preferred over silence.

---

## 5.4 Confidentiality

All confidential information shared by the Client will be handled responsibly.

Unless written permission is provided, TeerNetStudio will not intentionally disclose confidential files, passwords, unreleased branding, or sensitive business information.

---

## 5.5 Respect for Client Property

Client-provided assets such as logos, photographs, brand guidelines, videos, and documents will only be used for the agreed project.

These assets will not be redistributed, sold, or reused without permission.

---

## 5.6 Professional Advice

Whenever appropriate, TeerNetStudio may recommend improvements regarding:

* Layout
* Typography
* Colour usage
* Branding consistency
* Accessibility
* Print considerations
* Social media best practices

These recommendations are intended to improve the final outcome, while respecting the Client's final decisions.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

Good design is collaborative. Suggestions are never intended to replace your vision—they're meant to strengthen it.

---

## 5.7 Ethical Practice

TeerNetStudio will not knowingly create work that promotes:

* Illegal activities
* Fraud
* Copyright infringement
* Hate speech
* Harassment
* Discrimination
* Harmful misinformation
* Malicious or deceptive content

Projects that conflict with these principles may be respectfully declined.

---

## 5.8 Data Protection

Reasonable care will be taken to organize and protect active project files.

However, Clients are encouraged to securely store all delivered files after project completion.

---

## 5.9 Project Records

For quality assurance and future reference, TeerNetStudio may retain project records, including:

* Project Scope
* Quotations
* Invoices
* Final Deliverables
* Communication records related to the project

Confidentiality obligations remain in effect.

---

## 5.10 Continuous Improvement

TeerNetStudio is committed to continually improving its creative process, communication, documentation, and client experience.

Feedback is welcomed and helps improve future collaborations.

---

## 5.11 Equal Respect

Every Client deserves the same professionalism regardless of:

* Project value
* Business size
* Industry
* Experience level

Every project receives honest effort and professional treatment.

---

## Why This Matters

Choosing a designer means placing trust in someone with your ideas, your brand, and often your business.

This section reflects TeerNetStudio's commitment to earning that trust through professionalism, clear communication, ethical practices, and consistent quality.

---

## 📌 Important Note

Design is a collaborative service, not just a final product.

Our responsibility extends beyond creating visuals—we strive to provide guidance, transparency, and a reliable creative experience from start to finish.
