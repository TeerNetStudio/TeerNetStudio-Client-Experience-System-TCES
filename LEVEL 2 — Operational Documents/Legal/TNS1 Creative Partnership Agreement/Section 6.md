# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 6 — Communication Standards

---

## 6.1 Purpose

Clear communication is essential for every successful creative project.

This section establishes how TeerNetStudio and the Client communicate throughout the project to ensure clarity, efficiency, and professionalism.

---

## 6.2 Official Communication Channels

To keep project discussions organized, official communication should take place through one or more of the following channels:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* Discord
* Google Meet (Scheduled)
* Other mutually agreed platforms

Where possible, project-related decisions should remain within a single primary communication channel.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

Keeping all project communication in one place helps avoid confusion, missing information, and conflicting instructions.

---

## 6.3 Business Hours

Standard business hours:

```text
Monday – Saturday
10:00 AM – 8:00 PM (IST)
```

Messages sent outside business hours are always welcome, but responses may be provided during the next working period.

Business hours may occasionally vary due to holidays, travel, health, or unforeseen circumstances.

---

## 6.4 Response Time

TeerNetStudio aims to respond within:

| Communication Type | Estimated Response             |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| General Messages   | Within 24 hours                |
| Project Updates    | Within 24 hours                |
| Revision Requests  | Within 24 hours                |
| Urgent Issues      | As soon as reasonably possible |

These are response goals rather than guaranteed response times.

---

## 6.5 Meetings

Meetings may be arranged when necessary for:

* Project discussions
* Brand strategy
* Design reviews
* Clarifications
* Final presentations

Meetings should be scheduled in advance whenever possible.

---

## 6.6 Feedback Standards

To ensure efficient revisions:

Clients are encouraged to submit feedback as one organized list rather than multiple messages over an extended period.

Well-organized feedback helps reduce delays and improves project accuracy.

---

## 💡 Example

Preferred:

```text
Revision Round 2

1. Increase logo size
2. Change background colour
3. Replace second image
4. Correct phone number
```

---

## 6.7 Project Decisions

For clarity, important project approvals should be confirmed in writing.

Examples include:

* Project approval
* Final design approval
* Timeline changes
* Additional work
* Budget changes

Written confirmation helps prevent misunderstandings for both parties.

---

## 6.8 Respectful Communication

Both the Client and the Designer agree to communicate professionally and respectfully throughout the partnership.

Constructive feedback is always encouraged.

Personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, or abusive behaviour are not acceptable.

---

## 6.9 Emergency Requests

Occasionally, Clients may require urgent work.

While TeerNetStudio will make reasonable efforts to assist, emergency requests:

* depend on current availability,
* may require timeline adjustments,
* and may be subject to an express service fee if agreed in advance.

---

## 6.10 Communication Delays

If either party expects to be unavailable for an extended period, they should inform the other party whenever reasonably possible.

Early communication helps both parties plan effectively.

---

## 6.11 Language

Unless otherwise agreed, project communication will be conducted in English.

Minor discussions may take place in another mutually understood language if both parties are comfortable.

---

## 6.12 Respecting Time

Both parties agree to respect each other's working hours, scheduled meetings, and response times.

Professional communication contributes to a smoother creative process and a better working relationship.

---

# Why This Matters

Creative work depends on communication as much as design.

Clear conversations reduce misunderstandings, speed up approvals, improve collaboration, and help both the Client and the Designer stay focused on creating great work.

These standards are not intended to limit communication—they exist to make communication more effective.

---

## 📌 Important Note

Questions are always welcome.

There is no such thing as a "bad question."

Open and respectful communication is encouraged throughout the entire project.
