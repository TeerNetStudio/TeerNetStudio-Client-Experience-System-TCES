# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 7 — Revision Policy

---

## 7.1 Purpose

Creative work naturally evolves through feedback and collaboration.

This Revision Policy ensures that revisions remain organized, transparent, and fair for both the Client and the Designer.

---

## 7.2 What is a Revision Round?

A **Revision Round** is one consolidated set of feedback submitted by the Client after reviewing a draft.

A revision round may contain multiple minor changes submitted together.

Example:

```text
Revision Round 1

✓ Increase logo size

✓ Change headline colour

✓ Replace image

✓ Fix spelling

✓ Align text
```

This is considered **one revision round**.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

Collecting all feedback into one message speeds up revisions and helps prevent missed changes.

---

## 7.3 Minor Revisions

Minor revisions are included within the agreed revision allowance.

Examples include:

* Colour adjustments
* Font changes
* Alignment corrections
* Spacing adjustments
* Image replacement
* Text corrections
* Minor layout improvements
* Small icon replacements

These changes do not alter the approved creative direction.

---

## 7.4 Major Revisions

Major revisions fall outside the standard revision process.

Examples include:

* Completely new layout
* New visual concept
* Different design style
* Rebranding after approval
* Significant content changes
* New deliverables
* Major structural redesign

Major revisions may require:

* Additional quotation
* Updated timeline
* Revised Project Scope

---

## 7.5 New Concept Requests

Once a concept has been approved and work has progressed, requests to explore an entirely different creative direction will be treated as a **new design request**, not a revision.

---

## 7.6 Revision Allowance

The number of included revision rounds for each project will be specified in the **Project Scope (TNS-002)**.

Any additional revision rounds beyond the agreed allowance may incur additional charges.


Each service at TeerNetStudio includes a recommended default revision allowance.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, these defaults apply.

However, revision allowances may be customized within the Project Scope (TNS-002) to better suit the requirements of a specific project.

| Service                |                   Default Revision Rounds                  |
| ---------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------: |
| Logo Design            |                              3                             |
| Brand Identity         |                              3                             |
| Social Media Post      |                              2                             |
| Poster / Flyer         |                              3                             |
| Banner                 |                              2                             |
| YouTube Thumbnail      |                              2                             |
| Brochure               |                              3                             |
| Business Card          |                              2                             |
| Presentation           |                              3                             |
| Monthly Design Package | Unlimited **minor** revisions within the approved concept* |

This ensures consistency while allowing flexibility for unique projects.

---

## 7.7 Approval

When the Client confirms that a draft is approved, that version becomes the approved creative direction.

Subsequent requests that significantly change the approved design may be treated as new work.

---

## 7.8 Designer Corrections

Errors introduced by the Designer, such as spelling mistakes, formatting issues, or accidental omissions, will be corrected without affecting the revision allowance.

---

## 7.9 Client-Supplied Errors

Corrections resulting from inaccurate or incomplete information provided by the Client (such as incorrect phone numbers, prices, or text) may be treated as a standard revision or additional work, depending on the stage of the project.

---

## 7.10 Revision Response Time

To help maintain project momentum, Clients are encouraged to submit revision requests within a reasonable time after receiving a draft.

Extended delays may impact the overall project schedule.

---

## 7.11 Additional Revision Charges

If additional revision rounds are requested beyond the agreed allowance, the Designer will inform the Client of any applicable charges before continuing.

No additional work will begin without the Client's approval.

---

# Why This Matters

Revisions are an important part of the creative process.

This policy is not intended to limit creativity—it exists to ensure that feedback remains organized, projects stay on schedule, and both the Client and the Designer have a clear understanding of how changes are handled.

---

## 📌 Important Note

A revision improves an existing approved concept.

A completely new creative direction is considered a new project scope.

Understanding this distinction helps maintain fairness and project efficiency.
