# Welcome

## Welcome to TeerNetStudio 👋

Thank you for choosing TeerNetStudio.

Before we begin designing, we'd like to understand **you, your brand, and your goals**.

This questionnaire helps us gather the right information from the beginning so we can spend less time asking questions and more time creating meaningful work.

There are no right or wrong answers.

If you're unsure about any question, simply leave it blank or write **"Not Sure."** We'll discuss it together during the project.

Most clients complete this questionnaire in **10–20 minutes**.

---

## Why We Ask These Questions

Every successful creative project starts with understanding—not assumptions.

The more we know about your business, audience, and objectives, the better we can design solutions tailored to your needs.

This questionnaire is not a test.

It's the foundation of our creative partnership.
