# Purpose

This Creative Partnership Agreement establishes a clear understanding between **TeerNetStudio** ("Designer") and the **Client** before any project begins.

Its purpose is to ensure transparency, professionalism, and a smooth creative experience for both parties by defining responsibilities, project expectations, communication standards, payment terms, and ownership rights.

This agreement is intended to protect both the Client and the Designer while creating a respectful and collaborative working relationship.

# Guiding Principles

Throughout this partnership, both parties agree to uphold the following values:

* Professional communication
* Mutual respect
* Transparency
* Honest feedback
* Timely responses
* Fair collaboration
* Respect for each other's time
* Commitment to delivering quality work

---

# Definitions

For the purpose of this agreement:

**Designer** refers to TeerNetStudio or its authorized representative.

**Client** refers to the individual or organization requesting creative services.

**Project** refers to the creative work described in the attached **Project Scope (TNS-002).**

**Deliverables** are the final agreed files and assets to be delivered upon project completion.

**Revision Round** refers to one consolidated list of requested minor changes submitted by the Client after reviewing a draft.

**Approval** means the Client has confirmed satisfaction with a submitted version of the project.

**Business Day** = Monday–Saturday, excluding public holidays observed by TeerNetStudio.

---

# Agreement Scope

This agreement acts as the **master document** governing every creative project undertaken between the Client and TeerNetStudio.

Project-specific details such as deliverables, pricing, timeline, revisions, and file formats will always be defined separately in the corresponding **Project Scope (TNS-002).**

If any conflict exists between this agreement and the Project Scope, the Project Scope shall take precedence for that specific project.
