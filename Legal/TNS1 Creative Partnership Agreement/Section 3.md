# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 3 — Project Workflow

---

## 3.1 Overview

Every creative project at **TeerNetStudio** follows a structured workflow designed to ensure clarity, efficiency, and a professional experience for both the Client and the Designer.

Following this workflow helps minimize misunderstandings, reduce delays, and maintain consistent project quality.

---

# 3.3 Phase 1 — Discovery

Before any work begins, both parties discuss:

* Project goals
* Target audience
* Design style
* Deliverables
* Budget
* Timeline
* Questions and expectations

No design work begins during this stage.

---

# 3.4 Phase 2 — Project Scope

Once the project is understood, the Designer prepares a **Project Scope (TNS-002)** outlining:

* Services
* Deliverables
* Timeline
* Revision allowance
* File formats
* Pricing
* Exclusions

This document becomes the project's blueprint.

---

# 3.5 Phase 3 — Approval

The project officially begins only after:

* Project Scope is approved.
* Quotation is accepted.
* Creative Partnership Agreement is accepted.
* Required advance payment has been received (if applicable).

This ensures both parties share the same expectations before work starts.

---

# 3.6 Phase 4 — Creative Process

The Designer creates the agreed work according to the approved Project Scope.

During this stage:

* Regular updates may be shared when appropriate.
* The Client may be asked for clarification if additional information is required.
* Work is completed based on the approved scope.

---

# 3.7 Phase 5 — Review & Revisions

The Client reviews submitted work and provides feedback.

To ensure an efficient workflow:

* Feedback should be consolidated into a single message whenever possible.
* Revision requests should remain within the approved project scope.
* Major changes may require a revised quotation.

The complete Revision Policy is defined in **Section 7**.

---

# 3.8 Phase 6 — Final Approval

Once the Client confirms that the work meets the agreed requirements:

The project is considered **Approved**.

After approval:

* Only minor corrections (such as spelling mistakes caused by the Designer) will be corrected without additional cost.
* Requests for new concepts or significant design changes will be treated as new work.

---

# 3.9 Phase 7 — Final Payment

Where applicable, the remaining balance must be cleared before:

* Final high-resolution files
* Source files
* Editable files
* Commercial assets

are released.

---

# 3.10 Phase 8 — Project Delivery

The Designer delivers the agreed files according to the Project Scope.

Examples include:

* PNG
* JPG
* PDF
* SVG
* AI
* PSD
* MP4
* Other agreed formats

Only the formats specified within the Project Scope are included.

---

# 3.11 Project Completion

A project is considered officially completed once:

* Final files have been delivered.
* Outstanding payments have been completed.
* The Client confirms successful receipt of all agreed deliverables.

---

# 3.12 Future Support

Future assistance requested after project completion may include:

* Minor updates
* File conversion
* Additional exports
* New deliverables
* Further design work

Such requests may be quoted separately unless otherwise agreed.

---

# 📌 Important Notice

To maintain an efficient and professional workflow, TeerNetStudio follows the above process for every project.

Skipping workflow stages may not always be possible.

This ensures fairness, consistency, and the highest quality of service for every Client.
