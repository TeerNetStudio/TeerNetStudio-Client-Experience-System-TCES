# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 8 — Project Timeline & Management

---

## 8.1 Purpose

Every project follows an agreed timeline to help both the Client and the Designer plan effectively.

This section explains how project schedules are managed, how delays are handled, and what happens if a project is paused or becomes inactive.

---

## 8.2 Estimated Timeline

Project timelines are estimates based on:

* Approved Project Scope
* Timely client communication
* Timely approvals
* Required assets being received
* No major scope changes

Estimated timelines begin only after all project requirements have been fulfilled.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

A project cannot begin until the required information, assets, approvals, and agreed advance payment have been received.

---

## 8.3 Project Start Date

The official project start date begins when all of the following have been completed:

* Project Scope approved
* Quotation accepted
* Creative Partnership Agreement accepted
* Required advance payment received
* Required project assets received

---

## 8.4 Client Delays

If the Client delays providing:

* Feedback
* Approvals
* Images
* Logo
* Brand assets
* Text
* Required information

the delivery timeline may be adjusted accordingly.

The Designer will not be responsible for delays caused by missing client information.

---

## 8.5 Designer Delays

If TeerNetStudio anticipates any delay caused by:

* Illness
* Emergency
* Technical failure
* Unforeseen circumstances

the Client will be informed as soon as reasonably possible.

Where possible, an updated delivery estimate will be provided.

---

## 8.6 Project On Hold

A project may be marked as **On Hold** if progress cannot continue due to:

* Missing client feedback
* Missing required assets
* Outstanding approvals
* Client-requested pause
* Outstanding payments

During this period, project deadlines are automatically paused.

---

## 8.7 Inactive Projects

If no meaningful communication or required project activity is received from the Client for **30 consecutive calendar days**, the project may be marked as **Inactive**.

Before doing so, TeerNetStudio will make a reasonable effort to contact the Client using the agreed communication channel.

---

## 8.8 Project Closure

If a project remains inactive for **90 consecutive calendar days**, TeerNetStudio may close the project.

Project closure may require:

* A new booking
* A revised quotation
* Updated timelines
* A restart fee (if applicable)

This depends on the stage of the original project and current workload.

---
> **Business Day** = Monday–Saturday, excluding public holidays observed by TeerNetStudio. Eventually Same as Noraml Calender Holidays.
---
## 8.9 Restart Policy

Where a closed project is reopened after an extended period, previously completed work will be reviewed before continuation.

If significant time has passed or project requirements have changed, additional work may be quoted separately.

---

## 8.10 Rush Projects

Projects requiring completion within unusually short timeframes may:

* Depend on current availability
* Require adjustment to existing schedules
* Be subject to an express service fee

Rush requests are accepted at the Designer's discretion.

---

## 8.11 Timeline Changes

If either party requests changes that affect:

* Deliverables
* Project scope
* Revision volume
* Creative direction

the project timeline may be updated to reflect the additional work required.

Any significant changes will be discussed before implementation.

---

## 8.12 Project Completion

A project is considered complete once:

* All agreed deliverables have been provided.
* Final payment has been received.
* The project has been formally closed by TeerNetStudio.

Any future work will be treated as a separate project unless otherwise agreed.

---

# Why This Matters

Creative projects often involve multiple rounds of communication, approvals, and collaboration.

A clear timeline protects both the Client and the Designer by ensuring expectations remain realistic, organized, and fair throughout the project.

Timeline adjustments are not penalties—they are a practical part of managing professional creative work.

---

## 📌 Important Note

Project timelines depend on collaboration.

The faster decisions and required materials are shared, the smoother and faster the project can be completed.
