# 📘 TNS-001 — Creative Partnership Agreement

# Section 9 — Payment Policy

---

## 9.1 Purpose

This Payment Policy establishes clear expectations regarding project pricing, payment schedules, invoicing, and financial responsibilities.

Its purpose is to maintain transparency, prevent misunderstandings, and ensure a professional working relationship.

---

## 9.2 Project Pricing

All project pricing will be clearly outlined in the approved **Quotation (TNS-004)** before work begins.

The quotation will include:

* Services included
* Deliverables
* Revision allowance
* Timeline
* Project price
* Any additional agreed charges

No hidden fees will be introduced without prior discussion and Client approval.

---

## 9.3 Payment Schedule

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

| Stage                 | Payment       |
| --------------------- | ------------- |
| Before Project Begins | 50% Advance   |
| Before Final Delivery | Remaining 50% |

> "The required advance payment, as specified in the approved quotations as per **TeerNetStudio Client Experience System (TCES)**"

Alternative payment schedules may be agreed for larger or long-term projects.

---

## 💡 TeerTip

Advance payments reserve your project slot and allow work to begin with confidence for both parties.

---

## 9.4 Accepted Payment Methods

Accepted payment methods may include:

* UPI
* Bank Transfer
* Approved Payment Gateway
* Other mutually agreed methods

Available payment methods will be specified on the invoice.

---

## 9.5 Currency

Unless otherwise agreed, all quotations and invoices are issued in:

**Indian Rupees (INR ₹)**

For international clients, an alternative currency may be agreed before project commencement.

---

## 9.6 Additional Work

Work outside the approved Project Scope is **not automatically included**.

Examples include:

* New concepts
* Additional deliverables
* Extra revision rounds
* New design requests
* Rush delivery
* Additional export formats

Any additional work will be quoted and approved before commencement.

---

## 9.7 Late Payments

If payment is delayed:

* Final deliverables may be withheld.
* Source files may be withheld.
* Commercial usage rights may remain suspended until full payment is received.

TeerNetStudio will always attempt to resolve payment issues through respectful communication before taking further action.

---

## 9.8 Refunds

Refund requests are handled according to the **Refund Policy (TNS-005)**.

As creative work involves time, planning, and intellectual effort, completed work may not be eligible for a full refund.

Refund eligibility depends on the stage of the project.

---

## 9.9 Taxes

Any applicable taxes, where legally required, will be clearly indicated on the quotation or invoice.

---

## 9.10 Payment Confirmation

A project payment is considered complete only after funds have been successfully received and confirmed by TeerNetStudio.

Clients are encouraged to retain payment receipts for their own records.

---

## 9.11 Non-Payment

If outstanding payments remain unresolved:

* Project work may be paused.
* Delivery may be withheld.
* Future work may be declined until previous balances have been settled.

---

## 9.12 Financial Transparency

TeerNetStudio believes in transparent pricing.

Clients will always be informed in advance of:

* Additional charges
* Scope changes
* Rush fees
* New quotations

No additional charge will be applied without prior discussion and Client approval.

---

# Why This Matters

A clear payment process protects both the Client and the Designer.

It ensures that expectations are understood from the beginning, reduces misunderstandings, and allows creative work to progress without unnecessary financial uncertainty.

---

## 📌 Important Note

Payment is not simply for the final design.

It also covers the time, research, planning, creative expertise, communication, revisions, and project management invested throughout the creative process.
